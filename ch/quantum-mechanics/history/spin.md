# Spin

* Early 1910s: Experiments about emission spectra of alkali metals (transition between levels obey selection rules) and the Zeeman effect (strong magnetic fields can influence atomic spectra)

* 1924: W.Pauli proposed an additional degree of freedom, the spin, a "two-valuedness without a classical counterpart". This physical quantity is related to angular momentum. This physical quantity was also required to formulated the Pauli exclusion principle, and linking this quantity to symmetry properties of the wave function of systems with many indentical bodies.

* W.Pauli first introduced the new degree of freedom in the non-relativistic Schrodinger equation, later shown to be the non-relativistic limit of the relativistic quantum mehcanics equation by Dirac (1928).

* 1922-1927: Stern-Gerlach experiment. First performed to test Bohr-Sommerfeld atomic model, then used to verify and investigate the properties of spin.
