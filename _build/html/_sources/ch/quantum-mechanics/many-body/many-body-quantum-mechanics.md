[Quantum fields](quantum-mechanics:second-quantizazion:quantum-fields). From occupation number representation to space/momentum representation. One-body Examples in one-particle, two-particle, and many-particle systems. 

[Operators in quantum field theory](quantum-mechanics:second-quantizazion:operators). One-body operators. Two-body operators.
