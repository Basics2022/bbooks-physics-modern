# Examples

**Schwarzschild metric.** First Schwarzschild metric is derived, and some properties are discussed (Schwarzschild radius, event horizon,...). Then, the orbit of a light but non-zero mass object in a Schwarzschild metric is derived, and the **precession of perihelion** is discussed (precession of Mercury perihelion was used as a test for GR). Eventually, the trajectory of a zero-mass particle in a Schwarzschild metric is derived, and the **deflection of a light ray** is discussed (Eddington, 1919. Experimental measurements of the deflection of light during a solar eclipse).
