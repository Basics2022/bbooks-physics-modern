# General Relativity - Notes

* [**Differential geometry**](relativity-general:notes:differential-geometry): metrics, derivatives, curvatures. This could be a short version of the notes about [Differential Geometry](https://basics2022.github.io/bbooks-math-miscellanea/ch/differential-geometry/intro.html) and [Tensor Calclulus](https://basics2022.github.io/bbooks-math-miscellanea/ch/tensor-algebra-calculus/calculus-euclidean.html)

* **Equivalence principle** and the free fall as the natural state of motion: the physics in a freely falling laboratory is locally undistinguishable from physics in a gravity-free inertial frame in special relativity.

* [**Einstein field equation**](relativity-general:notes:einstein-equation). EFE is derived as the simplest linear relation between Ricci tensor - a second-order curvature tensor - and the energy momentum tensor, satisfying the energy-momentum conservation relation. [Linearization of Einstein equation](relativity-general:notes:einstein-equation:linearization) is performed to show the finite velocity of propagation: in the limit of weak gravitation, EFE can be recast as a linear wave equation with characteristic speed $c$.

* Examples:
  * Schwarzschild metrics
    * orbit of a planet, and precession of perihelion
    * trajectory of a zero-mass particle, and bending of rays of light
